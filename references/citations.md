# Citation formats

Three formats covered: APA 7 (default for English sport science), Vancouver (medical/biomedical English journals), GB/T 7714-2015 (Chinese journals and theses).

**Hard rule:** never invent author names, years, DOIs, page numbers, or journal volumes. If a reference is incomplete, ask the user. Do not guess to make the citation "look complete."

---

## 1. APA 7 (American Psychological Association, 7th edition)

Most common in: sport psychology, kinesiology, exercise science journals (*MSSE*, *JSS*, *IJSPP*, *Sports Medicine*, *Psychology of Sport and Exercise*).

### In-text citation

- One author: `(Smith, 2021)` or `Smith (2021) reported that…`
- Two authors: `(Smith & Jones, 2021)`. Narrative form: `Smith and Jones (2021)`
- Three or more authors: `(Smith et al., 2021)` from the first citation onward
- Multiple sources: `(Smith, 2021; Jones, 2020; Wang et al., 2019)`, alphabetical by first author
- Direct quotation: include page number `(Smith, 2021, p. 45)`

### Reference list

**Journal article:**
> Smith, J. A., Jones, B. C., & Wang, L. (2021). High-intensity interval training improves VO2max in untrained adults. *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*, *53*(4), 712–720. https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000002500

Notes: italic journal title, italic volume, regular issue in parentheses, en-dash in page range, DOI as full URL.

**Book:**
> Bompa, T. O., & Buzzichelli, C. (2019). *Periodization: Theory and methodology of training* (6th ed.). Human Kinetics.

**Book chapter:**
> Wang, L. (2020). Recovery strategies in elite athletes. In J. Smith (Ed.), *Sport science handbook* (pp. 145–168). Routledge.

**Thesis:**
> Chen, M. (2022). *Effects of cold-water immersion on muscle damage markers* [Doctoral dissertation, Beijing Sport University]. CNKI.

**Conference paper:**
> Liu, X., & Zhang, Y. (2023, July 5–8). *Acute effects of caffeine on sprint performance* [Paper presentation]. ECSS 28th Annual Congress, Paris, France.

### Reference list ordering

Alphabetical by first author surname. Multiple works by same author: chronological (oldest first). Same author + same year: append `a`, `b`, `c` to the year in both in-text and reference list.

## 2. Vancouver (numbered)

Used by: *British Journal of Sports Medicine*, *Journal of Sports Sciences*, most journals indexed by NLM/PubMed.

### In-text citation

Numbered in order of first appearance, in superscript or square brackets (check journal):
- `HIIT improves VO2max [1].`
- `Multiple studies confirm this finding [1,3,5–7].`

Numbers stay attached to the same reference throughout the paper.

### Reference list

Numbered in order of first appearance (not alphabetical).

**Journal article (≤ 6 authors, list all):**
> 1. Smith JA, Jones BC, Wang L. High-intensity interval training improves VO2max in untrained adults. Med Sci Sports Exerc. 2021;53(4):712–20. doi:10.1249/MSS.0000000000002500

**Journal article (> 6 authors, list first 6 then "et al."):**
> 2. Smith JA, Jones BC, Wang L, Brown K, Davis P, Lee H, et al. Cardiac adaptations to endurance training. Br J Sports Med. 2020;54(8):456–62.

Notes:
- Author initials with no periods, no comma between surname and initials
- Abbreviated journal title (use NLM Catalog), no italics
- No issue number is acceptable for some journals (check). When included, it goes in parentheses
- Page range abbreviated (`712–20` not `712–720`), a distinguishing feature of Vancouver

**Book:**
> 3. Bompa TO, Buzzichelli C. Periodization: theory and methodology of training. 6th ed. Champaign (IL): Human Kinetics; 2019.

**Book chapter:**
> 4. Wang L. Recovery strategies in elite athletes. In: Smith J, editor. Sport science handbook. London: Routledge; 2020. p. 145–68.

## 3. GB/T 7714-2015 (中国国家标准)

中文学术论文与中文学位论文必须遵守。文献类型标识：
- `[J]` 期刊文章
- `[M]` 专著
- `[D]` 学位论文
- `[C]` 会议论文集
- `[R]` 报告
- `[S]` 标准
- `[N]` 报纸文章
- `[OL]` 联机网络

### 文中引用

两种格式，由期刊决定：
- **顺序编码制**（最常用）：`[1]`、`[2,3]`、`[5-8]`，按出现顺序
- **著者-出版年制**：`(张三, 2021)`、`(张三 等, 2021)`

### 文后参考文献

**期刊论文 [J]：**
> [1] 张三, 李四, 王五. 高强度间歇训练对未训练成人最大摄氧量的影响[J]. 体育科学, 2021, 41(4): 23-30.

**英文期刊论文 [J]（在中文文献表中）：**
> [2] SMITH J A, JONES B C, WANG L. High-intensity interval training improves VO2max in untrained adults[J]. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise, 2021, 53(4): 712-720.

注意：英文作者姓全大写，名缩写不加点。

**专著 [M]：**
> [3] 邓树勋, 王健, 乔德才, 等. 运动生理学[M]. 3版. 北京: 高等教育出版社, 2015: 145-168.

**学位论文 [D]：**
> [4] 陈明. 冷水浸泡对肌肉损伤指标的影响[D]. 北京: 北京体育大学, 2022.

**会议论文 [C]：**
> [5] 刘晓, 张玉. 咖啡因对短跑表现的急性影响[C]//中国体育科学学会. 第十二届全国体育科学大会论文集. 北京: 中国体育科学学会, 2023: 234-235.

**网络资源 [OL]：**
> [6] 国家体育总局. 全民健身计划(2021-2025年)[EB/OL]. (2021-08-03)[2024-01-15]. https://www.sport.gov.cn/...

### 排列规则

- 顺序编码制：按文中首次出现顺序，**不**按字母或拼音排序
- 著者-出版年制：中文按拼音字母顺序，英文按字母顺序，中文在前英文在后（多数期刊）

### 常见错误

- 漏文献类型标识 `[J]`、`[M]` 等 → 必须有
- 页码用 `~` 或 `—` → 应用半角连字符 `-`
- 期刊名加书名号 `《》` → 不加
- 英文作者姓只首字母大写 → 应全大写
- DOI 写成 `https://doi.org/...` → 国标只要求 DOI 号本身（虽然加 URL 多数期刊也接受）

## 4. Common reference managers

The user will usually have references in Zotero, EndNote, Mendeley, or NoteExpress. Ask which they use and have them export in the appropriate style:

- **Zotero**: Tools → Preferences → Cite → install style from zotero.org/styles
- **EndNote**: Edit → Output Styles → APA 7th / Vancouver / Chinese Standard GB/T 7714 (-2015)
- **NoteExpress**: 中文用户最常用，自带 GB/T 7714-2015 样式

If the user pastes raw references, identify the format first and convert to the target. Do not silently switch styles.
